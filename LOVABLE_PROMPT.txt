## Iteration 5 — Professional floor plan visuals

Redesign the canvas and all element icons to look like a high-end enterprise office planning tool (think Archibus, Officespace, or a clean architectural floor plan). The app will be presented on stage at a conference and must look polished and professional.

### Canvas
Give the canvas a warm neutral background (beige/off-white). Grid lines should be subtle and unobtrusive.

### Element icons — pure SVG, no emoji
Replace all emoji icons with proper inline SVG illustrations drawn from a top-down (bird's-eye) perspective. Use clean architectural line art style — simple shapes, thin strokes, minimal fills. Every element should be immediately recognizable at a glance.

Important distinctions:
- `pult-skjerm`: a desk with a clearly visible external monitor (screen + stand visible from above)
- `pult-laptop`: a plain empty desk — NO laptop, NO device. Just the desk surface and chair.
- Meeting rooms: should show a table with chairs arranged around it, and a thin rectangle on one wall representing a display/screen.
- Decorative elements (plant, kitchen, toilet, printer): intuitive top-down drawings — use your best judgment to make them recognizable.

### Element palette (left sidebar)
Each palette item should show:
1. A small rendered preview of the actual SVG icon
2. The element name as a label

The palette should look professional — clean cards, subtle hover/selected states, category groupings with headers ("Arbeidsplasser", "Møterom", "Dekor").

### Overall aesthetic
Warm neutral tones, clean typography, generous whitespace. Enterprise-grade — not a toy app.

All UI text remains in Norwegian.

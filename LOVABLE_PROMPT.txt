## Iteration 5 — Redraw all floor plan elements as proper top-down SVG illustrations

Replace all emojis and icon-based rendering with hand-drawn-style inline SVG components. The result should look like a professional architectural floor plan tool.

---

### Canvas background
Change the canvas background color from the current dark/neutral to a warm beige: `#f0ebe0`.
Grid lines should be a subtle warm gray: `rgba(180,160,130,0.35)`.

---

### Element color palette
Every element has a background rectangle (the "footprint") using these fills:
- Workspaces (desks, lounge): background `#c8b89a`, interior lines `#5a4a3a`
- Meeting rooms: background `#b8c9b0`, interior lines `#3a5a40`
- Decor (plant, kitchen, toilet, printer, wall): each has its own palette (see below)

All background rectangles should have rounded corners (rx="4") and fill the cell area with 3px inset padding.

---

### Create component: `src/components/ElementIcon.tsx`

Export a single component:
```tsx
export function ElementIcon({ typeId, widthPx, heightPx }: { typeId: string; widthPx: number; heightPx: number })
```
It renders an SVG with `width={widthPx}` `height={heightPx}` `viewBox={`0 0 ${widthPx} ${heightPx}`}`.

Implement one case per typeId. Use pure SVG shapes — NO emoji, NO text, NO foreign objects.

---

### Drawing instructions per element type

#### `pult-skjerm` — Desk with external monitor (2×1 = 120×60px)
- Background rect: fill `#c8b89a`, rx=4, inset 3px
- Desk surface: rect centered horizontally, upper 60% of height, fill `#d4c4a8`, stroke `#5a4a3a` strokeWidth=1.5
- External monitor arc: a curved SVG arc path at the top edge of the desk, representing an ultrawide curved monitor seen from above. Draw as a single arc path with no fill, stroke `#2a2a2a` strokeWidth=2. Example: `M 30,16 Q 60,10 90,16` (adjust to fit)
- Chair: rounded rect below desk, centered, ~28×14px, fill `#9e8e78`, rx=5

#### `pult-laptop` — Desk with laptop (2×1 = 120×60px)
- Same background and desk surface as pult-skjerm
- Instead of monitor arc: a small open-laptop shape on the desk. Draw as two thin rects meeting at a slight angle (base + screen lid), fill `#3a3a3a` for screen, `#5a5a5a` for base
- Chair: same as pult-skjerm

#### `stapult` — Standing desk (2×1 = 120×60px)
- Background rect: fill `#c8b89a`, rx=4
- Desk surface: slightly wider rect, upper 55%, fill `#d4c4a8`, stroke `#5a4a3a`
- No monitor, no chair (standing desk)
- Add two thin vertical lines on desk surface suggesting a dual-monitor stand or height-adjust column

#### `lounge` — Lounge area (3×2 = 180×120px)
- Background: fill `#c8b89a`, rx=4
- Sofa: thick L-shaped or arc path at the bottom/left edges, fill `#a89878`, stroke `#5a4a3a` strokeWidth=1.5
- Small round coffee table: circle, fill `#b8a888`, stroke `#5a4a3a`, centered/slightly offset
- Optional: 1-2 small chair shapes (rounded rects) on open sides

#### `mote-lite` — Small meeting room 2-4 pax (3×3 = 180×180px)
- Background: fill `#b8c9b0`, rx=4
- Conference table: rounded rect, centered, ~100×50px, fill `#c8d8c0`, stroke `#3a5a40`
- Chairs: 2 small rounded rects above table + 2 below, fill `#7a9a7a`, rx=3
- Display/TV: thin rect (stroke only, no fill) on the top inner wall, ~60×6px — represents a wall-mounted screen

#### `mote-medium` — Medium meeting room 4-8 pax (4×3 = 240×180px)
- Same pattern as mote-lite but larger table (~140×60px)
- 3 chairs above + 3 chairs below + 1 on each short side
- TV line on top wall: ~90×6px

#### `mote-stort` — Large meeting room 8-16 pax (6×4 = 360×240px)
- Large oval or rect table centered (~200×90px), fill `#c8d8c0`, stroke `#3a5a40`
- 5 chairs along each long side + 2 on each short side
- TV line on top wall: ~140×6px

#### `telefonbod` — Phone booth (1×1 = 60×60px)
- Background: fill `#b8c9b0`, rx=4
- Simple phone handset shape (two circles connected by a curved line), stroke `#3a5a40`, no fill
- Or: a single person silhouette (head circle + slight shoulder arc) seen from above

#### `vegg` — Wall segment (1×1 = 60×60px)
- Solid filled rect: fill `#8a8070`, rx=2, full size minus 2px
- No internal drawing — walls are solid

#### `plante` — Plant (1×1 = 60×60px)
- Background: fill `#c8d8a0`, rx=30 (circular)
- Draw 4–6 leaf shapes radiating from center: elongated ellipses, fill `#5a8a3a`, stroke `#3a5a20`
- Small circle in center: fill `#8a6a40` (pot)

#### `kjokken` — Kitchen (4×2 = 240×120px)
- Background: fill `#e8dcc8`, rx=4
- Counter along top and one side: L-shaped rect, fill `#c8b890`, stroke `#7a6a50`
- Sink: circle cutout (white circle) on counter top section
- Coffee machine: small rect with a circle on top (mug), fill `#5a5a5a`, on counter
- Fridge: rect on one end, fill `#d0d0d0`, stroke `#9a9a9a`

#### `printer` — Printer (1×1 = 60×60px)
- Background: fill `#d8d8d0`, rx=4
- Printer body: rect ~40×30, fill `#c0c0b8`, stroke `#7a7a70`
- Paper sheet: thin white rect slightly protruding from top of printer body

#### `toalett` — Toilet/WC (2×2 = 120×120px)
- Background: fill `#dce8f0`, rx=4
- Draw 2 toilet bowls side by side: each is a rounded-bottom D-shape with a seat ring (two concentric D-paths), fill `#ffffff`, stroke `#5a7a8a`
- Small sink rect at top center: fill `#e8f0f8`, stroke `#5a7a8a`, with a circle drain
